FAQ

CS2 software: your questions answered

What is GCS Ware?

GCS Ware is lightweight software for Counter-Strike 2 with a clean in-game menu, cloud configs and a 6-hour update target after every CS2 patch.

How fast do I get access?

Instantly. Your key and download link are emailed the second payment clears. Most players are in a match within 5 minutes.

What happens when CS2 updates?

We aim to ship a fix within 6 hours of every Valve patch, but the timing isn't guaranteed, and GCS Ware may not work until the fix is out. The loader updates automatically, and we post status updates on Discord.

Will it lower my FPS?

Most players see no noticeable drop. GCS Ware is built lean, but performance can vary with your hardware and settings.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every plan (day, week or month) is a one-time payment with no auto-renewal. Need more time? Just grab another key.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with an Intel or AMD processor. Nothing else is needed, but virtual machines are not supported.

Can I get a refund?

The Dead-Key Guarantee covers a key that fails when it is delivered. Every other sale is final. Details are in our Refund Policy.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa and Mastercard cards, Google Pay and BLIK.

Still unsure? Ask us on Discord. We reply instantly, max 1 hour.
